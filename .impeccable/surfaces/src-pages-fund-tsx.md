# Development Fund

Scope: `/fund` below the shared site navigation. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: people evaluating Playblast who may later want to sponsor its maintenance and development. Job: understand the proposed funding model, planned recognition, and firm boundaries before the fund opens. Current action: explore Playblast on GitHub; secondary action: read the full terms in SPONSORS.md. Proof: the working product repository, planned recognition tiers, and explicit sponsorship exclusions. Constraints: the fund is pre-launch and checkout is not open; no invented activity, supporter counts, or funding metrics; sponsorship does not buy support, an SLA, hosting, installation, roadmap control, or priority treatment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show sponsorship as a transparent circuit around working software, not as an active pricing checkout. Refuse generic tier cards and empty fundraising theater.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black campaign field, warm-white display type, steel rules, and orange carried as one continuous signal through source, funded work, recognition, and the hard stop at purchased influence. Geometry remains square and editorial.

STORY: Open with an optimistic but explicit pre-launch position and a primary path to Playblast. Demonstrate how optional sponsorship would flow, present every planned recognition tier as a ledger, then state every exclusion in full before closing on the product.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A campaign-scale thesis occupies the left while a compact pre-launch status block and product action sit on the right. The first orange circuit line enters at the fold and leads directly into the funding model.

FORM: Funding Circuit, fourth in the grounded structural list, selected from surface seed `e83159dd`. The memorable moment is the signal visibly passing through maintenance and development while the influence branch terminates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
