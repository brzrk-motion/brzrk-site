# Contact

Scope: `/contact` below the shared site navigation. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: Playblast evaluators first, followed by existing users and general enquiries. Job: choose the right route, provide enough context, and open a prepared email draft. Action: open the draft in the visitor’s email client. Proof: the public contact address, transparent mailto behavior, and the GitHub issue route for reproducible bugs. Constraints: preserve the shared header and editorial-industrial system; do not promise response times, support, installation, hosting, or an SLA; no form data is sent to a brzrk server.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Contact is a handoff, not a marketing hero. Refuse the oversized preamble and put routing plus composition inside the first viewport.

OWN-WORLD: A near-black dispatch board built from hard rules, inset graphite fields, warm-white working copy, and orange reserved for the selected route and final dispatch action. Controls remain native, square, and visibly keyboard-operable.

STORY: Explain the mailto handoff in one line, route the enquiry, collect identity and context, then open the draft. Keep direct email and technical issue reporting visible as alternate routes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact left-aligned title occupies the upper-left while a direct-email rail sits opposite it. The bordered form board begins immediately below, with all three topic routes visible before the first text field.

FORM: Dispatch Board, fifth in the grounded structural list, selected from surface seed `e53eaa57`. The memorable moment is the selected topic row reading like an active dispatch route rather than a hidden dropdown value.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
