# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creative and technical teams evaluating focused software for production work. Playblast is aimed at small studios that need video review, client context, and invoicing tied to the same projects.

## Product Purpose

brzrk exists to build focused tools for the work behind creative production. Its public product is Playblast, which brings private video review and studio operations into one self-hosted workflow.

## Positioning

brzrk is an independent product company. Founder experience across software, motion, and graphic craft informs product judgment without positioning the company as a motion studio.

## Operating Context

The work spans versions, feedback, clients, estimates, retainers, and invoices. Playblast is a self-hosted MVP release candidate whose core workflow exists while clean-install and adoption checks continue.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public product focus is Playblast only.
- Products are described as free, open source, or self-hosted only where explicitly stated.
- brzrk does not promise hosting, installation, paid support, an SLA, roadmap control, or priority treatment.
- Internal infrastructure is not an external product.
- Public contact is `brzrk@brzrkmotion.com`.

## Brand Commitments

The company voice is precise, candid, independent, and product-focused. Public copy must avoid inflated adoption or production-readiness claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Shipped Playblast interface captures are available in `public/screenshots/`. There are no approved testimonials, customer counts, adoption numbers, performance claims, or additional public products, and future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Build focused tools around real production workflows.
- Let working software earn expansion rather than announcing speculative products.
- Keep product boundaries and release maturity explicit.
- Favor user control and self-hosting where the product states it.
- Operate independently and communicate without hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public surfaces must meet WCAG AA expectations, preserve keyboard access, respect reduced-motion preferences, and remain usable without horizontal overflow from 320px upward.
